Ziewise facility and briefing

The current safety, HR, finance, monitoring and print views are frames of the interactive facility. The mobile tripod and magnetic terminal were modelled from the company-confirmed ZIEVIEW product profile, revision 4. The facility, workers and information on the screens illustrate on-site workflows. Product authorship and material sources are documented in ../zieview/model/SOURCES.ko.md, ../workspaces/SOURCES.txt and ../equipment/CREDITS-ADDITIONAL.txt.

UR10e manufacturing inspection visualization

The six-axis Universal Robots UR10e visual geometry and joint-frame definitions are from the Universal Robots UR10e package in Google DeepMind's MuJoCo Menagerie. The package derives its visual model from the ROS-Industrial Universal Robots description.

The web version converts the original twenty OBJ material groups into indexed, quantized geometry. It regenerates crease-aware normals on the cast surfaces. It adds a fabricated pedestal, industrial optical tool and cable, physically based materials, and a six-joint inspection motion for this illustrative cell. The robot geometry is kept at its published metric scale.

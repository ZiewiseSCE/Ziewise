# Energy and D2C customer walkthroughs

The station geometry, product prototypes, screen graphics and animation were authored for the Ziewise walkthrough. Both interfaces show illustrative records. The energy HMI displays a demand forecast and a proposed storage schedule. The retail screens display an example consented browsing path and an operator's limited-audience review.

The wind-ridge and skincare-campaign images are the Ziewise redesign's existing energy and commerce editorial images. They are used as a distant landscape and a framed campaign photograph with a matching online storefront image.

All third-party surface maps below are reusable under CC0 1.0.

- pv-cell.webp and pv-rough.webp use the original base-color and roughness maps from Lennart Demes' **Solar Panel 002**. They were re-encoded for the browser. [Asset](https://ambientcg.com/a/SolarPanel002) · [Publisher license](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/)
- cream-stone.webp and cream-stone-rough.webp use diffuse and roughness maps from Rob Tuytel's **Marble 01**, reduced to WebP. The two honed-slab maps crop a single tile from the 4K original for the solid top of the consultation counter and its sample plinth. [Asset](https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01)
- quarter-oak.webp and quarter-oak-rough.webp reuse the site's prepared maps from Jenelle van Heerden's **Mocha Oak Veneer**. [Asset](https://polyhaven.com/a/mocha_oak_veneer)
- service-concrete.webp uses the existing site's prepared base-color map from Dimitrios Savva's **Hangar Concrete Floor**, reduced to WebP. [Asset](https://polyhaven.com/a/hangar_concrete_floor)
- [Poly Haven license](https://polyhaven.com/license) · [CC0 1.0 legal text](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode)

Keep the four JavaScript modules, the eleven WebP files, and this source record together. Supply the tour's Three.js namespace to buildIndustryA with the energy or commerce ID. The scenes take normalized progress, and their ready promise resolves after the local images load.

Industry vignettes for ZiewPrint and ZiewObserver

Local assets

The A3 duplex multifunction printer, laptop, terminal, authenticated sheet,
operations desk, articulated displays, rack enclosures and all room geometry
were modeled for this experience. The interface graphics and paper document
are original illustrative compositions. The sample service, staff ID, events,
addresses and measurements were written specifically for the walkthrough.

server-faces.webp is a resized derivative of the existing Ziewise site asset
assets/rack-front-photo-v1.webp. Each physical server module receives its own
corresponding portion of the photograph. The surrounding chassis, rails, power
lamps, connectors, patch leads and diagnostic console are individual meshes.
https://ziewise.com/assets/rack-front-photo-v1.webp

oak.webp and oak-normal.webp are resized maps from Mocha Oak Veneer, photographed
by Jenelle van Heerden for Poly Haven. The material is used on the desks and
joinery. CC0 1.0.
https://polyhaven.com/a/mocha_oak_veneer

weave.webp is a resized, neutral-colour derivative of Poly Wool Herringbone
by colormass and Rico Cilliers. It supplies the fine floor fabric. CC0 1.0.
https://polyhaven.com/a/poly_wool_herringbone

paper-normal.webp derives from Paper 001 by Lennart Demes at ambientCG. It
supplies the fine relief of the paper sheet and paper stock. CC0 1.0.
https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Paper001

window-daylight.webp is cropped from the project's derivative of Derelict
Airfield 02 by Alexander Scholten for Poly Haven. It appears behind the
printer-room glazing. CC0 1.0.
https://polyhaven.com/a/derelict_airfield_02

Delivery

Keep the MJS and six WebP images together. Call buildIndustryB(T,'print') or
buildIndustryB(T,'observer'), add its group and call update(p) with 0–1
progress. The three local-space anchors correspond to source, assessment and
operator result. Await ready for an offline capture. Each scene can be batched
with the existing batchStatic helper before awaiting ready. The host supplies
environment lighting and the camera. To place only the equipment inside an
existing factory bay, add the returned equipment objects to another group
before batching. They retain all product animation references. The print
equipment contains the source laptop and multifunction printer. The monitoring
equipment contains the working server rack and operations desk. The review
folder holds rendered proof images and is optional at runtime.

Hangar slab

Hangar Concrete Floor by Dimitrios Savva, published by Poly Haven.
https://polyhaven.com/a/hangar_concrete_floor
https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/png/1k/hangar_concrete_floor/hangar_concrete_floor_diff_1k.png
https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/png/1k/hangar_concrete_floor/hangar_concrete_floor_nor_gl_1k.png

hangar-slab-basecolor.webp is the seamless 1K scan lifted to a cool neutral coating. It retains the underlying pits, scratches and scuff patterns. hangar-slab-normal-gl.webp is the corresponding tangent-space OpenGL normal, vector-resampled to 768 square and losslessly encoded after tangent quantization. Source scale is 2 by 2 metres per tile.

Set the base color to sRGB and leave the normal at Three.js NoColorSpace, with both axes wrapping in RepeatWrapping. A starting material is white tint, roughness 0.66, metalness 0.035 and normalScale (0.62, 0.62). The architecture module's workshop slab already has world UVs at 3.45 metres per unit. A texture repeat of 1.725 on both axes reproduces the scanned 2 metre scale. Leave the line markings on their own geometry.

Fastened wall liner

Corrugated Iron 03 by Charlotte Baglioni, published by Poly Haven.
https://polyhaven.com/a/corrugated_iron_03
https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/png/1k/corrugated_iron_03/corrugated_iron_03_diff_1k.png
https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/png/1k/corrugated_iron_03/corrugated_iron_03_nor_gl_1k.png

fastened-sheet-basecolor.webp is the neutral-light graded sheet with the original fastener rows. fastened-sheet-normal-gl.webp preserves the original OpenGL tangent orientation. Both maps are 1024 square. The liner is vertically fluted when V runs upward. The native sample covers 2 by 2 metres. The module's profiledWall geometry already maps one tile in 2 metres.

Use the same basecolor and normal color-space settings as the slab. Start with white tint, roughness 0.59, metalness 0.34 and normalScale (0.30, 0.30) over the existing coarse rib geometry. Mipmaps and anisotropy 4 to 8 help long grazing views.

Exterior through the glazing

Derelict Airfield 02 by Alexander Scholten, published by Poly Haven.
https://polyhaven.com/a/derelict_airfield_02
https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/4k/derelict_airfield_02_4k.hdr

daylight-yard-horizon.webp is a globally tone-mapped, mildly desaturated crop of the photographed 360-degree HDR panorama. The daylight sheds and vegetation form a distant conceptual campus outlook. The original source is 4096 by 2048. This band retains image rows 546 through 1182, or approximately +42 to −14 degrees latitude. The output is 4096 by 637. Horizontal U covers the full circle, with the horizon three quarters of the way down from the upper edge.

Map the strip once around an inward-facing spherical band using thetaStart 48 degrees and thetaLength 56 degrees, centered horizontally on the visitor at eye height. Keep the band within the camera far plane and behind the 3D facility. MeshBasicMaterial with an sRGB map, toneMapped false and fog false preserves the prepared outdoor exposure. Replace the synthetic distant buildings and sky cards to expose the photograph through the windows. The roof, walls, glazing, mullions, equipment and walking surface keep their original 3D geometry.
inspection-local-rgbe.png and desk-local-rgbe.png are local reflection probes baked at the working height of the inspection lens and the finance workstation. Each uses six floating-point views of this tour, resampled into an equirectangular RGBE map, plus a contribution from the credited Machine Shop 02 environment. No customer premises or data were captured.
